FORMAT SPLIT AND HOW WE COUNT

24 originals a month. 20 is the floor, not the target.

The monthly split

Reels and Shorts (original)8
Statics (original)6
Carousels (4–6 slides)4
UGC and creator-style3
Topicals and moment-jacks3
Total originals24

Plus 12–15 Stories frames, X text posts and all platform adaptations -
none of which are counted.

The counting rules

  • One original = one unique concept, shot or designed once
  • Adaptations are free. Resize, re-crop and re-caption for
    another platform are not counted - up to three per original
  • A carousel is one asset regardless of slide count. Past six
    slides it counts as two
  • A reel re-edited into a Short with a new hook and title is an
    adaptation, not a new asset
  • Stories frames are not counted and are included in the
    retainer
  • Shortfall in any month rolls into the next; we do not count filler
    to hit a number

Net effect: 24 originals produce roughly 70–80 published units a month across the four platforms.
